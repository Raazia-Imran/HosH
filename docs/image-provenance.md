# Website image provenance

The service and industry `*-color.webp` photographs in `src/assets/` were made by AI assisted colorization of photographs extracted from the client supplied company profile. The unused grayscale source copies were removed during handoff cleanup and remain available in Git history; the colors are reconstructed and are not verified as the original scene colors. The Training circle reuses the colorized `service-training-color.webp` photograph.

The site describes what each image depicts. It does not claim that a photograph shows a specific HosH project or employee. Replace an image with an approved original color photograph when one is available. The homepage industrial scene and inspection object graphic were already part of the project before this revision.

## Social sharing card

`public/og-image-v2.jpg` is a 1200 by 630 JPEG for Open Graph and Twitter cards. Its illustrative refinery and blueprint background was generated with the imagegen tool on 29 October 2026. The prompt requested a navy and cyan industrial scene, the supplied HosH logo and tagline, the service line INSPECTION | INTEGRITY | TRAINING and hoshintegrity.com, without metrics or accreditation claims. The generated image was resized and packaged with the exact original `public/hosh-logo.png` composited over its logo area, so the final logo is the supplied asset rather than a generated imitation. The background is not evidence of a HosH facility, employee or completed assignment. The earlier `public/og-image.jpg` remains available unchanged.

## Sharing card revision without an address

The active card is now `public/og-image-v3.jpg`, 1200 by 630 pixels. On 29 October 2026, the user requested removal of the printed website address while approving the remaining artwork. Built in imagegen edit mode was used with the prompt: remove only the entire cyan hoshintegrity.com address line, fill its area with the existing navy background, and preserve the logo, PREVENTING FAILURES tagline, service line, industrial scene and palette. A small retouched background patch from that output was packaged over the address area of the previous repository card to preserve the supplied logo and all remaining artwork. The earlier v2 card and original card are historical assets; active metadata, documentation previews and checks use v3. Real domain URLs remain in canonical, sitemap and sharing metadata. No website address is printed on the active artwork.
